**Full description (long description):**

Botanisht is a private, local-first garden companion that helps you grow
better — without giving up your data. Your plants, your care history, and
your preferences stay on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no
subscriptions. No tracking.

FEATURES

• Smart plant catalog — 121 herbs, vegetables, houseplants, and pollinator
  flowers, each with care requirements, pet-safety info, and companion
  planting guidance.

• Multi-zone garden — organize plants into Indoor, Kitchen, Hydroponic,
  Pollinator, and Outdoor zones, each with its own conditions and view.

• Care tracking — watering, fertilizing, pruning, measurements, and health
  status with progress over time.

• Companion & Clash alerts — see which plants help each other thrive and
  which conflict, automatically checked against your own garden.

• Hydroponic log — track pH, TDS, pump cycles, temperature, humidity, and
  light hours for your hydro setup.

• Diagnostics dashboard — health and environment insights across your whole
  garden at a glance.

• Garden Score (Pro) — earn XP, levels, and care streaks for every plant,
  plus a full expanded catalog. Botanisht Pro is unlocked on the honor
  system: use it freely, and if it helps your garden, consider a donation.

PRIVACY FIRST

Botanisht is fully local-first. Everything runs on your device and nothing
is uploaded anywhere. Optional severe-weather advisories use a zip code you
enter yourself — no GPS, no location permissions.
